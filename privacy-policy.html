Privacy Policy for AI WhatsApp Bot Services

Last Updated: January 15, 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our AI-powered WhatsApp bots, we collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Messages you send to our AI bots
  • Any information you provide during conversations (product inquiries, service requests, etc.)
  • Conversation history for customer service and AI improvement purposes
  • Business-specific data relevant to the service you're inquiring about

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide AI-powered customer service and support
  • Respond to your inquiries with relevant business information
  • Process service requests and provide recommendations
  • Improve our AI assistant's responses and accuracy
  • Maintain conversation context for better service

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is:

  • Stored securely using industry-standard encryption
  • Retained only as long as necessary for service provision
  • Never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Protected by secure cloud infrastructure

4. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of communications at any time

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to provide our AI WhatsApp bot platform:

  • WhatsApp Business API: For message delivery (Meta/Facebook)
  • OpenAI: For AI-powered customer service responses
  • MongoDB Atlas: For secure conversation data storage
  • Cloud Hosting: For reliable 24/7 bot operation

6. Multi-Business Platform

Our platform serves multiple businesses through AI-powered WhatsApp bots. Each business has its own:

  • Dedicated WhatsApp phone number
  • Customized AI personality and knowledge base
  • Isolated conversation data
  • Business-specific response templates

7. Data Retention

We retain your conversation data for up to 2 years to provide ongoing customer support and improve our AI services. Business owners may have access to their customer conversations for service purposes.

8. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in various countries where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy. We will notify you of significant changes via WhatsApp or through the business you're communicating with.
